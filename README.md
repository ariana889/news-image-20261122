One image that sums up the world news of Sunday 22 November 2026: the COP31 climate talks in Antalya collapsing with no deal after the EU rejected a draft that never mentioned fossil fuels, Iran saying two supertankers struck naval mines and caught fire east of the Strait of Hormuz, the search for dozens of missing workers after the Cikande candy-factory fire in Banten, a Delta Air Lines system outage delaying more than 1,600 flights across the US on the Sunday before Thanksgiving, the first trilateral summit of Trump, Xi and Putin in Shenzhen, and the World Court finding Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya. The repository holds the image as `entry.png`; `main.ts` copies it to the output directory unchanged.

## The six stories

1. **COP31 climate talks collapse** — Antalya, no deal; the EU rejected a draft with no mention of fossil fuels.
2. **Iran: tankers hit mines in Hormuz** — two supertankers ablaze east of the strait, Tehran says.
3. **Factory fire: dozens still missing** — the search at the burned Cikande candy factory in Banten, Indonesia.
4. **Delta outage delays 1,600+ flights** — a weight-and-balance system failure on the Sunday before Thanksgiving.
5. **First Trump–Xi–Putin summit** — the three leaders meet in Shenzhen and sign a nuclear-risk declaration.
6. **Court: Myanmar committed genocide** — the World Court's first finding of state genocide, over the Rohingya.
